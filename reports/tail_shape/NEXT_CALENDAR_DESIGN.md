## One bounded replication, if pursued

Use **SPX raw OHLC with VIX**, one next-session target, and one three-column calendar block. This provides a matched underlying for the broad SPX policy/release channel and avoids adding another asset/horizon search. The target is next observed session's fixed GK-floor-plus-raw-overnight variance: intraday Garman–Klass with the existing `1e-10` floor, plus `log(open_next/close_entry)^2`. Call it a full-session **daily OHLC risk proxy**, not measured high-frequency integrated variance or an identified announcement contribution.

Use a positive mean forecast with the already tested regularized log-link proper-loss objective. The market baseline must retain strict 1/5/22 own total-risk means, 1/5/22 negative-return histories, lagged VIX level, VIX9D/VIX shape and VVIX, entry weekday, and nominal elapsed hours. Every market feature ends at the previous observed SPX close. Compare baseline plus the joint `(cpi_plan,nfp_plan,fomc_plan)` block against both this baseline and the historical mean on exactly the same admitted rows. There are **two primary contrasts**, not six separate event searches; individual coefficients cannot earn independent event claims. Do not add countdowns, interactions, or post-event arms in this replication.

Keep source eligibility unchanged: each document's publication civil date must be **strictly before the previous observed session date**. Use the same selected BLS ledgers and complete original annual FOMC schedule; no new acquisition, inferred missing year, revised realized date, or substituted source is needed. Each civil month touched by the predictor window must have an eligible explicit plan for **both** BLS series; the nominal ending year must have its full eligible original FOMC schedule. Unknown coverage excludes the row for every model, including the mean. Existing exclusions cluster in some periods, so this sample cannot be described as complete market-history coverage.

## Window definition and frequency

For an entry date `t` known now, set the nominal window to **16:00 Eastern on t through 16:00 Eastern on the next Monday–Friday civil date**, skipping weekends only. CPI/payroll flags indicate an eligible original planned timestamp inside this open-left, closed-right interval. The FOMC flag indicates that nominal ending date equals an original meeting's planned final **date**; do not invent a historical statement clock. This extends the existing [`nominal_window`](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/src/macro_plan_features.py:36) convention without consulting the next observed market date. Compute duration in UTC to preserve DST changes.

This remains a nominal policy. Early closes still use 16:00; holidays are not skipped. Good Friday can be a nominal endpoint, and a Friday before a Monday holiday still ends nominally on Monday. The actual next observed SPX session determines only the risk target and its close-time maturity. Do not filter or redefine predictors after checking whether the future nominal date became an actual session.

**Extending the endpoint need not increase flag frequency.** Where the admitted BLS plan clock is 08:30, the timestamp is inside both the prior overnight window and this full-session window. Wave 4 already uses the same FOMC final-date indicator. Thus this proposal principally changes the modeled risk interval, not the fact that FOMC plans are included. Source exclusions, simultaneous plans, month-boundary coverage, nominal holidays, and completed-label fences determine scored frequency; eight original Fed plans per year does not imply eight retained event rows. No new frequency has been computed here.

## Next decision

One limited replication is defensible to test whether the corrected information policy and full-session target change the old calendar result. It should not be advertised as discovery of a previously untested economic channel. Preserve development 2016–2019 with labels complete by 2019-12-31, evaluation from 2020-01-02 with labels complete by 2025-10-20, minimum 1,000 common mature training labels, monthly expanding fits, and the sealed boundary. Refit dates must depend on available features, never future label existence. Training support after adding VIX9D and the strict known-month mask has not been computed here; a source/date eligibility check must establish that the fixed initial training requirement is feasible before committing to the run, without lowering it or silently dropping controls.

Before implementation, freeze both contrasts, the existing absolute proper-score improvement standard rather than weakening it, cumulative multiplicity allocation, dependence-aware uncertainty, and stability requirements. Prewritten tests must establish source eligibility, unknown-month propagation, retained canceled plans, excluded emergency meetings, early-close/holiday/DST conventions, and invariance of predictors to future observed-calendar mutations. Any passing finding would concern documentary original plans on this archival proxy/sample; it would not show that policy announcements caused the variance or that a tradable event strategy exists.
