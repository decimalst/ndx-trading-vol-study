# Commodity option-implied risk: new-source admission plan

The completed claims study remains a verified negative result with 142 cumulative comparisons. This prospective direction asks whether the joint oil/gold ETF option-implied volatility block adds information to five-session QQQ variance forecasts. No individual-index screening, latent factor selection, model grid, commodity feature panel or predictive score has been inspected.

Repository review found prior tests of pooled GLD/USO return stress and signed cross-asset returns, but no OVX/GVZ forecast inputs or matched commodity realized-risk controls. The independent prospective review selected one fixed block: delayed log OVX and GVZ implied daily-variance units. The matched model adds each ETF's delayed signed one-session return, its square, and log strict five- and 22-session mean squared returns. This yields market12, matched20 and candidate22 columns. Zero signed returns and daily squared returns are valid; zero rolling mean risk leaves its logarithm unavailable. No epsilon or clipping repairs it.

Prices and option indexes are first aligned to the full QQQ reference calendar and delayed one observed session, without filling missing dates. Both indexes were live by the end of 2008; use a prospective source floor of 2009-01-02 and numerical ceiling of 2025-10-20. Index launch dates do not authenticate unchanged historical downloads. Document the 2022 quotation-source and 2025 strike-selection changes, preserve the historical series as supplied, and do not select favorable subperiods.

The exact daily download identities are the links published on [Cboe's historical-data page](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/vix_historical_data): `https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/daily_prices/OVX_History.csv` and the corresponding `GVZ_History.csv`. Acquisition saves unmodified bytes, HTTPS response receipts and hashes privately. Inspect only the header before writing numerical-parser tests. A strict tested parser must validate the complete date column before converting any in-scope value; it must not convert or expose values outside the declared bounds. Source validation is separate from later forecast registration.

The economic channel is a hypothesis. [BIS Working Paper 420](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-420-correlation-between-commodity-and-equity-returns-implications-portfolio-allocation) studies joint commodity/equity forecasting, while [BIS Working Paper 619](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-619-volatility-risk-premia-and-future-commodities-returns) studies commodity-related risk premia and returns. Neither establishes that these two delayed option indexes improve this repo's QQQ variance baseline. Implied option risk includes pricing and risk-premium effects; it is not the same object as future realized ETF variance or a causal macroeconomic shock.

This source review is not a completely unseen external-information environment; future documentation must not claim otherwise. This plan does not register a forecasting hypothesis. The family remains at 142 until new code, source admission, a fixed protocol and prewritten verification pass their prospective checks.
