## Identity and limits of the proposed information

Cboe's current methodology identifies GVZ with GLD options and OVX with USO options, each targeting 30-calendar-day expected volatility. Thus the precise proposed input is an ETF option-implied risk state. It is not a direct measure of spot gold/oil uncertainty, and its maturity differs from the five-session QQQ variance target. A predictive relationship across those assets and horizons is a hypothesis. [Official Cboe methodology, sections 1.1 and 2.1](https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/governance/Volatility_Index_Methodology_Selected_Broad_Based_Index_Equity_and_ETF_Volatility_Indices.pdf).

The current methodology includes changes after the experiment's source ceiling and does not authenticate every historical vintage. An official January 27, 2025 notice explicitly lists OVX and GVZ in the change effective February 10, 2025: options with a zero ask are excluded in addition to those with a zero bid. Source admission should record this known change rather than assume a constant historical calculation. This review does not establish the complete change history, launch/live-history boundary, immutable vintages or exact historical daily publication times. [Official Cboe change notice](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/release_notes/2025/Modifications-to-the-Strike-Selection-in-Volatility-Index-Calculations.pdf).

## Controls needed for an incremental claim

Keep the original 12-column baseline intact. Its pooled cross-asset stress compresses identity and sign: a gold move and an oil move can produce the same aggregate, and today's standardized return magnitude does not represent each asset's multi-session variance state. Merely beating that baseline would therefore mix commodity-specific historical risk with option-implied information.

The matched arm should add **separate GLD and USO signed-return histories and backward-looking variance histories**, with exactly the same eligible rows in every arm. Root's prospective design is reviewing one/five/22-session signed returns together with squared one-session return and log mean squared returns over five/22 sessions. This retains zero one-session returns without taking log zero; an entirely zero or undefined longer variance window still requires a predeclared rule. These are close-return second-moment proxies, not intraday realized variance. There is no fresh evidence here about their support or usefulness.

The existing VXN, VIX and VIX9D/VIX inputs must remain in both controls. They condition on the already available broad/technology equity option state. Conditional improvement still would not prove that commodity IV is independent of all nonlinear global stress or volatility-of-volatility information. VVIX is already a tested source; adding it as a nuisance control would be a prospective benchmark choice, not a newly discovered source, and must not be chosen after seeing the commodity results.

Authenticate the GLD/USO price field and corporate-action convention before treating the matched histories as exact underlying returns. Prior [source review](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/reports/overnight_index/SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md) and [index-input audit](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/reports/iterative_signal_search/INDEX_OPPORTUNITIES.md) document an adjusted-close preference with a raw-close fallback and incomplete per-column adjustment provenance. No OHLC range estimator can be inferred from a close-only cache. Reusing that cache should preserve its explicit proxy limitation rather than silently upgrade its meaning.

## Narrow first family and interpretation

Root selected one **joint OVX/GVZ block**, with two formal comparisons: candidate versus matched commodity-history control and candidate versus baseline12. Require both to pass. The latter prevents success from depending on deterioration of the enlarged matched control; the former tests option information conditional on the declared commodity histories. A passing joint block would not establish that both individual indices help. Do not select OVX or GVZ separately after viewing coefficients or scores.

Use the established fixed monthly log-target OLS and causal separate smearing; no estimator or memory grid is necessary to test the new source. Final transforms, history cutoffs and support gates belong in the prospective design, not this audit. For daily Cboe fields, a complete prior-QQQ-session delay is the conservative default pending source authentication. Align to the uncompressed QQQ calendar before delay, retain unavailable dates, and do not turn absent daily observations into indefinitely carried states. Preserve the source ceiling 2025-10-20, protected period from 2025-11-03, full five-session target windows and prior-session training-label maturity.

A log-IV/realized-variance spread is not automatically a variance risk premium: trailing realized risk is not the contemporaneous physical expectation of future variance. In an unpenalized linear model already containing the realized control, subtracting that same control from a log-IV column only changes the basis; it does not create another information channel. Likewise, projecting IV on matched controls with training-only coefficients can describe remaining variation, but residualization is not independent evidence or a separate discovery. Never subtract future commodity realized variance in a forecast input.
