## Visible allocation categories, units and definitions

The 2016 and 2025 competitive result pages share the following visible structure. Each amount column is labeled **Tendered** or **Accepted**, with dollar signs and comma-separated amounts. No thousands/millions scale heading is displayed. The pages are consistent with full-dollar presentation; this review does not convert the values or validate a machine mapping against XML.

- The upper table distinguishes Competitive, Noncompetitive, FIMA (Noncompetitive), Subtotal, SOMA, and Total. SOMA is shown separately after the public subtotal.
- The lower table distinguishes Primary Dealer, Direct Bidder, Indirect Bidder, and Total Competitive, again with separate Tendered and Accepted columns. The intended dealer-allocation inputs reside in the **Accepted** column of this lower table, with **Total Competitive** as the competitive denominator. Subtotal, overall Total, offering amount, tendered bids, and NCR tentative totals have different meanings.
- Footnote 6 defines primary dealers as submitters bidding for their own house accounts. Footnote 7 defines direct bidders as non-primary-dealer submitters bidding for their own house accounts. Footnote 8 defines indirect bidders as customers placing competitive bids through direct submitters, including foreign and international monetary authorities bidding through the Federal Reserve Bank of New York. Routing a customer's bid through a dealer therefore does not make that customer award the dealer's own-account category.
- Footnote 4 labels a published bid-to-cover calculation using the upper-table subtotal. Its displayed numerator/denominator are not the dealer-share pair. Footnote 5 labels TreasuryDirect awards on the 2016 page and Treasury Retail awards on the 2025 page; the different label should be preserved by a source mapping rather than silently treated as proof of an unchanged interface.
- High/median/low yield and interest rate are displayed with percent signs, while Price is separately labeled and accrued interest is explicitly per $1,000. Those displays are not allocation amounts. Footnotes 1–3 describe acceptance of lower-yield tenders and the median/low-yield distribution conventions; no yield or allocation computation was performed here.

The NCR page instead labels **Amount Tendered**, with Noncompetitive and FIMA (Noncompetitive) rows. Its warning about final results is part of the source contract. This three-page check confirms the visible categories and definitions in these instances, not uniform templates throughout 2010–2025 or any exact numerical accounting identity.
