## Findings that affect the experiment

- Some advertised two-year or five-year auctions actually reopened securities originally issued at longer tenors. The 18 realized cases need explicit announcement-to-final-result identity reconciliation. Original tenor must remain distinct from the advertised remaining term.
- A conditional warning does not necessarily name the eventual substituted security. The April 2017 warning named one possible five-year security, but the final notice confirmed a different original seven-year security. Final identities must be established from actual results and confirmations.
- Archive links can overstate applicability. One January 2019 notice names a two-year note and a bill, while the index also links a five-year note. The ledger preserves that association without treating it as body-supported.
- January 2015 differences between initially advertised and final auction dates are explained by a later weather rescheduling notice. Both dated stages are retained.
- The June 2010 correction changes SOMA and overall totals, expressly leaving other result details unchanged. The pilot retained the original amounts. The other affected original results still require matching.
- The September 2015 settlement-rounding disclosure expressly declares the originally released auction results final. It is not a replacement dealer-allocation release.
- The June 2019 contingency auction must be excluded from regular auctions and historical comparison pools. Two strict header-parser failures were retained and resolved manually from complete originals.
- The unavailable December 2019 original has a separate provisional indexed-text lead. Its original remains unavailable; this lead does not authenticate a recovered PDF or establish what security was ultimately issued.

## Next experiment and remaining work

The proposed hypothesis is that unusually high primary-dealer competitive allocation at Treasury auctions may contain information about subsequent QQQ volatility beyond existing market and bond-price controls. The proposed measure compares dealer allocation share with preceding eligible auctions of the same original tenor. This hypothesis has not yet been registered, fitted or scored.

The next step is to complete auction announcement/result history reconciliation, including realized security substitutions, original-tenor handling, offering-size controls, release timing and explicit missing-source treatment. Then freeze the forecasting protocol and run the prewritten checks before evaluating predictive performance. Existing strict identity helpers and frozen results remain unchanged; new reconciliation behavior belongs in separately tested code.

Reviewing all known linked notices does not prove the index contains every relevant historical notice. Source limitations remain separate from predictive failures. The protected later market-data window remains unexamined, and the completed earlier experiment family is not reopened.
