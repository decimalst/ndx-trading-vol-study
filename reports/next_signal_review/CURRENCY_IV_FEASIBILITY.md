# EVZ currency option-implied uncertainty: source and novelty review

**Decision: reject EVZ as the next continuing-source experiment.** Cboe ceased the index before the U.S. open on **January 27, 2025**, so it cannot supply an ongoing signal through the existing October 20, 2025 research ceiling or subsequent untouched confirmation. This is a source-availability rejection, not a predictive null result. No comparison is registered here; the completed research family remains 144. The exact effective date is established by Cboe's [January 13, 2025 cessation decision](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/release_notes/2025/20250113_Consultation-Results-Regarding-the-Cessation-of-the-RVOL-and-EVZ-Indices.pdf), rather than inferred from a last historical observation.

This review inspected documentation, source-code definitions, protocol text, and filenames only. No raw EVZ/FXE history was requested, downloaded, decoded, or scored; no market cohort, support count, feature, fit, or result was calculated. Admitted external evidence below is from official documents. Secondary search snippets were not admitted as evidence. Existing code, protocols, and reports were left unchanged.

## Matched controls and why UUP is insufficient

The existing fetch contract contains only HYG/TLT/GLD/USO/UUP, with adjusted-close preference and raw-close fallback; see [fetch.py](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/src/fetch.py:35) and its [field-selection code](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/src/fetch.py:75). FXE is absent from that declared cache. No raw values were read to establish coverage or adjustment quality.

UUP follows a **basket** of major currencies against the U.S. dollar, as described in the issuer's [official fund document](https://www.invesco.com/us-rest/contentdetail?contentId=75b8e01e98630410VgnVCM10000046f1bf0aRCRD). It is not the same exposure as a euro trust: its other currencies, basket construction, and futures/funding components are not removed by squaring its returns. UUP realized-risk controls are derivable in principle from an existing declared series, but they cannot establish that an EVZ effect comes from forward euro risk rather than ordinary FXE price risk.

If a separate historical-only inquiry were ever authorized, the minimal matched block would require newly authenticated FXE prices: prior-session signed log return, its square, log strict five-session mean squared return, and log strict 22-session mean squared return. This would define market12, matched16, and candidate17 with one EVZ input, using the same rows and fixed causal timing for all arms. Both candidate-versus-matched and candidate-versus-market comparisons would be required. Existing equity-IV controls and UUP composite remain present. This is a conditional source requirement, not a registered model or permission to acquire prices. Missing prices, all-zero log-risk windows, launch floor, adjustments, and cessation must stay explicit.

**Next action:** stop EVZ admission for the current continuing-source objective. A future currency-IV proposal needs an authenticated source that still publishes, plus a matching underlying-price control and an independently established historical contract. Replacing EVZ, substituting UUP, or shortening the period would constitute a new prospective design decision rather than a repair to this rejected source.
