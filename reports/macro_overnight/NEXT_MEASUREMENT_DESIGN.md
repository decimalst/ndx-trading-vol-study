# Prospective measurement-quality memory experiment

This is a bounded design proposal, **not a registered wave or model admission**. It uses the already audited Risk Lab SPY response and existing audited market controls. No new data acquisition, numerical source inspection, model fit, or predictive comparison was performed for this note. Earlier frozen sources, protocols, and results stay unchanged.

The concrete question is whether the provider's reported uncertainty width changes the persistence of a recent measured volatility move. A move accompanied by a wider relative interval may be a less persistent measurement. The competing explanation is that the width itself marks a different volatility state. A control containing the width as a main effect separates those two questions. This adds an auxiliary measurement channel; it does not repeat the earlier kernel-to-RV ratio or select another latent regime after its results.

Use one asset, one primary measurement, one forecast horizon, and one interaction. The primary measure remains the **SPY trade-QMLE** selected before the measurement audit. Let `v_s` be its native volatility level, `c_s` its recorded confidence half-width, and `q_s = v_s²`. The saved provider chart code places its interval at `v_s ± c_s` and displays both in volatility percentage units. Thus `u_s = log1p(c_s / v_s)` is a dimensionless recorded relative-width feature. The audit does not establish a confidence level, standard-error interpretation, calibrated coverage, or observation-error variance. Do not divide the width by an assumed normal quantile, manufacture quarticity, or call this HARQ or a Kalman filter.

Define `H_w(s) = log(mean(q_j, j=s-w+1,…,s))` for `w=1,5,22`, requiring all positions on the fixed reference calendar. Define the measured innovation `z_s = H_1(s) − H_22(s)` and the single memory interaction `u_s × z_s`. The interaction coefficient is unconstrained: its sign is a result to inspect after the fixed test, not a restriction that presupposes the proposed mechanism.

| Model | Fixed inputs |
|---|---|
| Historical mean | Mean of the exact common matured training targets; unconditional control |
| Strong baseline | Constant; own-QMLE `H_1,H_5,H_22`; log VIX; log(VIX9D/VIX); log VVIX; means of negative QQQ adjusted close-to-close log returns over 1/5/22 sessions; entry weekday Tuesday–Friday indicators |
| Width control | Baseline plus `u_s` |
| Quality-dependent memory | Width control plus the single `u_s × z_s` interaction |

QQQ return leverage is an available broad-market control, not SPY's own return history. A pass would establish an increment over these fixed controls, not over every possible same-asset price model. There is no estimator-ratio feature, quote-estimator selection, confidence-width threshold, chosen regime, extra horizon, or interaction search.

Use the same fixed positive log-link estimator for the three regression models: mean `log(h)+q/h` plus `0.01` times the standardized slope squared norm, an unpenalized intercept, training-only population feature scaling, and training-mean target scaling with exact restoration of forecast units. The existing deterministic Newton/Armijo implementation can be reused after new contract tests. Reject zero-scale inputs and nonconverged fits for the entire family; no fallback, feature deletion, clipping, or post-score penalty change. Require at least 1,000 completed common training labels. Refit monthly at the first feature-complete origin, independently of whether that month's first future label is ultimately observed.

The primary target is the **next reference-session squared provider-native annualized volatility** `q_(t+1)`. This is a new ETF measurement target. It is not Oxford daily variance, integrated daily variance, an overnight variance component, or a cash-profit target. Do not infer an annualization factor or divide by 252. Squaring would be an explicit new-model transformation; the completed measurement audit itself did not square or convert the source. A common constant change of volatility units rescales `q` and positive forecasts together and shifts raw proper scores by a constant, leaving paired gaps unchanged. That mathematical property does not establish a constant provider annualization or revision process.

Proposed conservative archival timing is as follows. At reference-session close `t`, Risk Lab measurements and widths use only dates through the second preceding reference session, `t−2`. Cboe closes and existing daily return controls use only the preceding session, `t−1`. Assign a target measurement a maturity date two reference sessions after its own dated observation, and admit training labels only when that maturity date is at or before the preceding session of the monthly fit origin. This is a declared historical-information delay assumption, **not verified historical publication latency**. Exact provider session boundaries, publication times and revisions are still unresolved; no finite chosen lag certifies point-in-time availability. Any result remains retrospective research pending actual source-vintage evidence or a prospective capture period.

Use source numeric values only through 2025-10-20. Proposed development origins are 2016-01-04–2019-12-31, with target maturity no later than 2019-12-31; evaluation origins are 2020-01-02–2025-10-17, with target maturity no later than 2025-10-20. Those maturity fences will remove the last origins of each phase; do not shorten the target or invent later maturity dates. Fixed evaluation stability slices are 2020–2022 and 2023–2025. All models share every training and scored row. Preserve missing source dates before rolling or shifting, using the bounded observed QQQ session labels as an explicit common-US-equity reference, not a certified historic SPY calendar. Future realized dates may determine labels and their maturity, never predictors or monthly refit origins.

The audited source omits seven reference dates during 2016–2019 and twenty during 2020–2025-10-20, including the whole 2018-02-05–2018-02-09 week. These gaps and their trailing-window effects must remain. No result can establish behavior on the omitted stress week. The audit's complete-window counts reset at displayed period boundaries and are not model sample counts; calculate the actual training and scored counts only after prewritten contracts pass, without changing the 1,000-row minimum.

Register **nine contrasts**: the quality-dependent-memory forecasts versus each of the historical mean, strong baseline, and width control, evaluated against three fixed measurement series. The first series is trade-QMLE squared. The other two are squared five-minute-trade and fifteen-minute-trade estimates from the same audited provider response. Fit every model to the primary target only; the two alternatives evaluate the exact same frozen forecasts, with no calibration or refitting. Include all nine contrasts in multiplicity bookkeeping, including failures. The auxiliary comparisons test whether a forecast ranking survives alternative recorded measurements. They cannot prove unbiased measurement, latent-volatility accuracy, or independent-source replication.

For a concrete prospective gate, use paired absolute proper-score gaps, not percentages of possibly negative scores. Require the primary interaction model to beat all three controls by at least `0.005` in both development and evaluation, with negative gaps in both fixed evaluation slices. Require negative gaps for all three controls and both phases on each alternative measurement. Use the established 21/63/126 retained-observation circular-block bootstrap and HAC126 inference, **99,999 bootstrap draws**, and the maximum phase p-value. Preserve the existing telescoping allocation `alpha_w = 0.05 / (w × (w+1))`: wave five receives **`0.05 / (5 × 6) = 1/600`**. Apply Holm nine at that wave allocation, plus Holm across the full accumulated family at `0.05`; if the current macro wave retains all six hypotheses, that cumulative count would be 93.

The first and strictest wave-Holm cutoff is `(1/600)/9 = 1/5400`. Require the minimum attainable Monte Carlo p-value with the plus-one correction `1/(B+1)` to be below one tenth of that cutoff, namely `1/54000`. With 49,999 draws, the minimum is `1/50000`, which is too coarse. With 99,999 draws, it is `1/100000`, which meets the fixed resolution rule. The wave cutoff is stricter than the first cumulative-Holm cutoff `0.05/93`, so it governs this draw requirement. Freeze the allocation, draw count, seed, family and failure handling before the first new association is read. This note does not spend that allocation or alter any existing registered wave.

Prewritten tests should cover the exact field mapping and raw-byte hash; date filtering before numerical parsing; common constant unit changes; zero width without an invented floor; the relative-width and interaction formulas; complete 1/5/22 reference-session windows across absent dates; two-session Risk Lab lags; future-measurement mutations leaving current predictors unchanged; target maturity and phase fences; feature-selected monthly refits; all-arm sample identity; primary-target-only fits; unchanged forecasts under alternate-label evaluation; and complete nine-contrast failure bookkeeping. Independent reconstruction must verify features, training masks, optimizer conditions, predictions and paired inference before any candidate is described as a lead.

HARNet is not an input to this proposed experiment. Its audited extension adds 397 SPX dates through 2020-01-31, agrees with the old prefix only within tolerance, and lacks the old Parzen kernel field. It cannot supply an unchanged kernel-arm replication or a 2020–2025 confirmation panel. Mixing it with SPY to manufacture a long common target would also combine different assets and unresolved units. Any later Oxford extension must retain the exact old prefix and append only genuinely new dates under a separate frozen replication design.

The source remains research-only and quarantined. The maximum defensible favorable claim is that a specified recorded uncertainty interaction improves historical prediction of a specified provider-native SPY measurement on the observed common sample, beyond the listed controls, with the declared delay assumption. It would warrant prospective measurement capture and further validation. It would not establish a deployable trading signal, a calibrated measurement-error model, or a recovered latent volatility process.
