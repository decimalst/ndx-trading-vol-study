In particular, the proposed sequential age stage can retune residual ridge shrinkage even when age lies in the nuisance span. The original 17-column baseline includes its intercept; the matched depth/window baseline has 20 columns including its intercept.

The new 252-close requirement changes admitted training and application cohorts. Refit all controls on the new common rows; old saved hinge forecasts cannot serve as matched controls. Monthly scheduling must precede query-label availability filtering. Save and independently verify complete application predictions as well as the scored subset, closing the older runner's gap in storing only scored predictions.

## Literal feature and target definition

Retain the full bounded observed SPX reference-session calendar, unique, ordered, timezone-naive and normalized. It comes from the pinned raw OHLC source before dropping missing observations. At origin position i, let k=i-1. For i>=252, the price window is exactly positions k-251 through k inclusive, or equivalently i-252 through i-1: 252 closes and 251 return intervals. All must be positive finite stored float64 closes. No expanding history, all-time-high state, calendar-day interpolation, approximate tie, forward fill, compressed missing window or alternative window is allowed.

Let P be the exact maximum stored close in this window and j the latest reference position whose stored close equals P exactly. Define integer age q=k-j, A=q/251, D=log(P/C[k]), D2=D*D, and U=log(C[k]/C[k-251]). Record q, peak position/date, window endpoints and cutoff independently of numerical features. A is in [0,1], D and D2 are nonnegative, and U is signed. Equal closes give exact zero log ratio. An expiring old maximum can cause an age reset without a new high; A is the age of the most recent maximum inside this rolling window, not the duration of an economic recovery or an all-time-high spell.

Use the original 15 raw baseline columns in their declared order: const, I, R, ret_d, ret_w, ret_m, ret_q, lr_d, lr_w, term, lvvix, entry_dow_1, entry_dow_2, entry_dow_3, entry_dow_4. Their formulas and one-observed-session market lag remain those of `index_hinge.yaml`. Keep training-centered I_square and R_square. Add exactly D, D2 and U as nuisance slopes; age is used only by the final scalar stage. No age/depth interaction, sign restriction or threshold is added.

The sole target is h=21: y[i]=log(C[i+21]/C[i]), with target_end and available_date both the close of reference session i+21. Retain the original raw-price-index target calculation; zero and negative targets are valid. Do not construct a 63-session target. Current-origin close C[i] is a target denominator, never a feature input. Mutating a future endpoint can change its label but must not alter a previously issued prediction. There is no assumed execution at an observable C[i] when the feature cutoff is i-1.

Initial insufficient windows and genuine missing-source windows are marked unavailable on their original calendar rows. A common complete row requires all 15 original raw features plus A/D/D2/U. Missing rows may make an originally defined row unavailable, but they cannot shorten or slide its 252-session definition. At least 1,000 common mature rows are required at every declared monthly fit. Arithmetic invalidity on otherwise complete observed inputs is a trial failure, not a reason to relabel a row as missing and silently drop it.

## Prospective finite arithmetic

For the new D/U log ratios, avoid an overflowing raw division or cancellation from subtracting two large logarithms. Specify this one float64 algorithm for positive finite x,y. If x==y return literal 0. Otherwise obtain `(mx,ex)=math.frexp(x)` and `(my,ey)=math.frexp(y)` and d=ex-ey. If abs(d)<=1, return `math.log1p((math.ldexp(mx,d)-my)/my)`. Otherwise return `math.fsum([math.log(mx/my), d*math.log(2.0)])`. Mantissas and this small exponent adjustment are bounded; no unscaled extreme ratio is formed. Require a finite result with the exact stored-value ordering's sign; a nonidentical pair returning zero is an arithmetic failure. P/C[k] consequently cannot produce negative depth. Form D2 once from the returned D and reject nonfinite values or nonzero multiplication collapsing to zero.

This is a prospective arithmetic definition for new predictors, not a change to any frozen calculation. The independent feature oracle should use a separately implemented high-precision log ratio on generated extreme/near-equal cases before implementation is accepted. Standard float64 rounding remains; equality and peak selection always use stored prices, never log values. Assert scale invariance only when the chosen positive scaling preserves the stored ordering/ties and finite representation. Exact reversible powers of two are a strong test; arbitrary rounded rescaling can merge two unequal stored prices and is not a promised bitwise invariant.

Use the original float64 training mean/population-standard-deviation conventions for nuisance transforms. All declared nuisance scales must be finite and strictly greater than 1e-12; do not delete, zero-score or reweight a failing nuisance column. Squared centered I/R, standardized designs, Gram/rhs systems, predictions, residuals, losses and required gradients must be finite. Numerical over/underflow that invalidates a required nonzero operation fails the trial. A valid constant-age stage is handled explicitly below and is not a nuisance-scale exception introduced after inspection.

## Common monthly cohorts and sequential model

Keep the original source ceiling 2025-10-20 and protected start 2025-11-03. Origins remain 2016-01-04 through 2025-10-17; development is 2016-01-04 through 2019-12-31 with target availability by 2019-12-31; evaluation is 2020-01-02 through 2025-10-17 with targets by 2025-10-20. Evaluation slices remain 2020-01-02 through 2022-12-31 and 2023-01-01 through 2025-10-17. These fences imply a shorter scored tail for h=21; do not extend target access to fill it.

For each month with common feature-complete applications in these origins, fit on its first such application, even if that query's target is unscored. Let c be the preceding observed SPX session. Include every earlier common-complete origin whose finite target is available no later than c. Use the same exact training index, target vector and application index for all four arms. Monthly coefficients remain frozen for all common-complete applications in that month; query features use each query's own previous-session cutoff. No target outcome may choose the fit date, application membership, transform, model or penalty. If a monthly fit has fewer than 1,000 common mature rows, fail the registered trial rather than begin at a favorable later month.

Arm `mean` predicts the common training target mean. Arm `baseline` fits the original 17-column baseline. Arm `depth` fits the same baseline plus D, D2 and U. Each nuisance ridge fit uses training-only I/R curvature centers, training-only population standardization of its slopes, an unpenalized intercept equal to the training target mean, and objective mean squared error + .01 times standardized slope squared norm. The original baseline's means/scales are identical to the corresponding matched columns because the cohorts are identical. Fit separately; do not freeze the weaker baseline's coefficients when fitting depth.

For arm `peak_age`, freeze the complete depth model first. Apply that same current monthly depth model to its admitted mature training rows to obtain f0; these are in-sample current-fit predictions, not past issued forecast errors. Let age center m be `math.fsum(A_train)/n`, except exact-constant ages use the first stored age exactly. Set a=A_train-m. Keep age scale one; do not standardize it to variance one. Set r=y-f0 and

`beta = (math.fsum(a_i*r_i)/n) / (math.fsum(a_i*a_i)/n + .01)`.

Issue `peak_age = depth_query + beta*(A_query-m)`. There is no new intercept and no joint refit of nuisance coefficients. Exact-constant training age has canonical beta=0, even if query age differs; retain all observations and copy the depth probability-independent return prediction exactly. A nonconstant stage with exactly zero numerator likewise has beta=0 and exact copied depth predictions. Reject nonfinite products/reductions/coefficients and nonzero-product or nonzero-coefficient underflow. The derivative of one half of the specified scalar objective is `mean(a*(f0+beta*a-y))+.01*beta`; verify it independently.

This is sequential conditional correction, not Frisch–Waugh–Lovell residualization and not a joint penalized optimum. If a=X*gamma+u for standardized nuisance X, then its training numerator contains `gamma'*(X'r/n)` in addition to `mean(u*r)`. Ridge gives `X'r/n=.01*b` rather than zero at the nuisance optimum. Thus beta can be nonzero even when u=0. A successful comparison against depth may reflect useful shrinkage adjustment within already supplied information. Do not interpret beta, a matched-origin feature fixture or a successful MSE gate as proof of conditional statistical independence. No residualized-age alternative is part of this trial.

The producer uses the closed normal-equation ridge solve and scalar formula. The independent verifier uses an augmented least-squares nuisance solve and a one-column augmented least-squares scalar solve conditional on the saved, independently validated depth fit. Independent optima must not replace saved producer predictions. Carry forward the hinge numerical tolerances: saved and independent ridge/scalar half-gradient <=1e-10, coefficient relative1e-7/absolute1e-12, independently reconstructed forecasts relative1e-8/absolute1e-12. Freeze any additional transport/audit tolerances before historical admission and exercise them on generated ms/us/ns Parquet/JSON roundtrips.
