# Prospective scoring and execution contracts

The two protocol tests, eight initial score tests and six execution tests were written before their modules existed; actual missing-module failures are retained. The later empty-panel classification contract first failed against the initial scorer, then passed after the missing-support disposition was corrected. All of this occurs before claims market-cohort admission, numerical support inspection, fitting or scoring.

The scorer reuses the preserved round2 circular-bootstrap, Bartlett-HAC and Holm numerical primitives. Its new independent verifier reconstructs the statistical calculations without importing those producer functions. Both contrasts share random resampling designs within a phase. Support is checked on the entire paired family before an effect can qualify: insufficient phase, distinct-release, slice or offset counts invalidate both comparisons. Effects are absolute QLIKE differences. Every sign, effect, stability and multiplicity gate is required as specified in `claims_release.yaml`.

The new runner authenticates the original source audit, independently verified first-report ledger, four exact market snapshots and the previous complete 140-comparison family. It preserves previous comparison records and source identities. Its source reader authenticates all byte snapshots and preflights date columns before bounded numerical decoding. Historical price/feature calculations occur only inside the callback that follows durable registration of both hypotheses and all inherited records.

A complete run contains two registrations, 140 inherited records and two evaluated records. Any registered support, numerical, verification or execution exception keeps both comparisons as unevaluable p=1; support failures retain their distinct `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` label. Existing attempt files prevent a second callback or silent overwrite. Forecasting outputs are kept in a separate private subdirectory, preserving completed source-audit artifacts.

Before the prospective freeze, the runner executes the generated AR(1) calibration and its independent reconstruction, then the full repository suite. It checks that code is unchanged during testing and binds code, protocol, sources, prior reports, calibration and test evidence. There is no retrospective threshold repair. The frozen runner independently verifies both all issued forecasts and the complete scored comparison family before publishing successful metrics.

The source ledger and its independent admission are complete. The prospective protocol and execution components do not themselves establish sufficient market support, statistical power or predictive value. An eventual gate pass remains an exploratory lead requiring future untouched evaluation.
