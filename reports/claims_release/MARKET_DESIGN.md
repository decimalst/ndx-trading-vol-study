Claims release study: established market baseline and five-session target contracts

This task owns only the new market helper, its generated tests and this evidence. The formulas are read from the established src/orthogonal_round2.py baseline without importing that producer or loading any market values, sources, support counts, fits or scores. Existing ledger/source files and frozen experiments remain unchanged.

The API `build_market_features(daily, cross, iv)` returns exactly the twelve-column MARKET tuple, followed by rv_total, on the unchanged complete daily DatetimeIndex. MARKET is const, lrv_d, lrv_w, lrv_m, lev_d, lev_w, lev_m, liv, lvix, term, xasset_stress, market_stress. Inputs contain exactly daily open/high/low/close/adj close/volume, cross hyg/tlt/gld/uso/uup, and IV vxn/vix/vix9d. Column order may differ. Auxiliary calendars may omit observations; alignment to the daily calendar never fills them.

All input calendars are checked before numerical conversion or logarithms. They must be unique, increasing, timezone-free midnight DatetimeIndexes without NaT, with no dates after October 20, 2025. The daily and target calendars must be nonempty; empty auxiliary calendars mean unavailable constituents. Millisecond, microsecond and nanosecond date transport must preserve results. Only real numeric column dtypes are accepted, including nullable numeric missingness. Observed prices, cross prices and IV must be strictly positive, infinity is invalid, and volume cannot be negative. Zero volume makes logged volume undefined. Observed OHLC geometry must be consistent even when another price is unavailable. No source date or row is filtered to make validation pass.

Daily total variance is Garman-Klass intraday variance clipped at 1e-10 plus squared raw log(open / previous close). Adjusted close affects leverage only. HAR inputs are log variance today and the log arithmetic mean of the last 5 and 22 variances. Leverage is the negative part of today's adjusted log return or of its 5/22-session arithmetic mean, with clipping after aggregation. VXN, VIX and VIX9D are first aligned to the daily calendar and then shifted one observed session. The IV features are log VXN, log VIX and log(VIX9D/VIX).

Prior standardization uses the preceding 252 full calendar rows, at least 126 nonmissing observations, a shifted historical mean and ddof=1 standard deviation. A zero prior scale remains undefined. Cross-asset stress is RMS of the five standardized log returns with skipna=False. Market stress is RMS of the positive parts of standardized log volume and standardized overnight share; overnight share is clipped to [0,1]. Missing constituents never become zero or trigger forward filling. No unused candidate or VVIX calculation is included.

The API `make_five_session_targets(rv)` returns exactly y and target_end on the unchanged full RV calendar. Each y is the arithmetic mean of the next five observed calendar rows, with skipna=False; target_end is the fifth subsequent row's date, even if a constituent value is missing. The last five rows lack complete targets. Genuine missing RV is allowed, but observed RV must be finite and strictly positive. Neither current variance nor values after the endpoint may affect that row's target.

The prewritten generated suite includes independent scalar/NumPy formula oracles, raw versus adjusted prices, the intraday floor, HAR arithmetic versus log means, post-aggregation leverage clipping, alignment before IV lag, the past-only 252/126 z-score, missing constituent propagation, zero scale, positive-part stress, future mutation and prefix invariance, full-calendar endpoints, missing targets, malformed calendars and values, ceiling-before-arithmetic checks, date transport units and no input mutation.
