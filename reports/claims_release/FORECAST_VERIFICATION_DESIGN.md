# Independent claims forecasting reconstruction

Prospective design, 2026-09-08, before this verifier's implementation or access to a historical market cohort. The interface is `verify_forecasts(daily, cross, iv, ledger_rows, config, produced)`, returning `VERIFIED` with independently established counts or raising a mismatch. Only the new verifier, separate generated test file and these logs are owned here. No producer claims feature, model, market or pipeline routine is imported or called. The independently verified source ledger is a separately pinned boundary; opaque source-comparison values must never enter forecasts.

The independent reconstruction preserves the complete daily calendar and derives the twelve established market features plus five-session target from the draft and MARKET_DESIGN.md. Daily raw-OHLC intraday Garman–Klass variance is floored at1e-10, then squared raw overnight log return is added. HAR variance means are arithmetic before logging; leverage is clipped after its adjusted-return mean. All three IV series align to the daily calendar before a one-position delay. Cross/market standardization uses only the preceding252 calendar positions, at least126 observed values and sample standard deviation. No missing constituent is filled or reweighted. The target is the mean of exactly the next five variances and ends at the fifth next observed session, even when a constituent is missing.

Claims reconstruction selects the latest strictly earlier displayed DOL release before looking at its value. It retains the selected reference/release dates and raw age even when stale or unresolved. Civil ages1–7 are eligible; age8 is stale. Current and four exact preceding Saturdays must each be observed and released strictly before origin. The feature is log current first count minus the mean of the four previous log first counts; the matched level is that mean, claim_age is age/7 once, and Tuesday–Friday origin indicators are known throughout. The unresolved2017 ledger value and each missing chain remain unknown without fallback. Comparison-side counts are opaque.

The scheduling config contains origin_start/origin_end, development/evaluation date pairs, source_end and positive minimum_train/minimum_train_releases. Root's frozen production values are1000 and200; smaller values appear only in invented fixtures to make timing tests bounded. Every application arm uses the same complete19-column rows. Monthly fitting starts at the first complete requested origin before querying its future label. Earlier complete training rows require finite positive target and target_end no later than the previous observed daily session. Monthly train/application identities and distinct latest-release counts are exact. Full-calendar position modulo5 is assigned before filtering. Phase-unassigned complete origins remain unscored applications. Every requested origin retains coverage metadata; missing or unscorable rows are never compressed away.

The model reconstruction computes its own population means/scales and standardized design, enforces every nonconstant scale>1e-12 and declared full rank under singular-value relative cutoff1e-12, then fits each arm through reduced QR and a triangular solve. This differs from the producer's SVD least-squares solve. Each arm independently uses all mature residuals for stable exact log-mean-exp Duan smearing. Nonfinite/nonpositive arithmetic invalidates verification; no clipping, penalty or fallback exists. Named coefficients, means/scales, singular values/rank, normal-equation audit, train/application counts, all scored and unscored predictions, and QLIKE are reconstructed. The numerical normal-equation statistic is max absolute standardized-design transpose times residual divided by training count.

Prospective tolerances are coefficients atol1e-7/rtol1e-7, predictions atol1e-12/rtol1e-7, and other numerical comparisons atol1e-12/rtol1e-9. Calendar, cohort, columns, status, count and provenance identities are exact. Finite arithmetic and explicit missingness are checked separately from numerical closeness. These tolerances are fixed before historical execution and must not be relaxed to fit its outputs.

The producer payload has applications, panel, coverage and schedules DataFrames plus a fits list. Applications preserve all complete requested origins; panel preserves all three scored arms. Fits store month, ISO fit/cutoff dates, exact train/application ISO lists, train/release counts and per-arm model audits. Coverage carries complete/missing feature lists, selected claim metadata, target/phase/scoring reasons and fit linkage. Schedules carry every requested month, including no-complete-origin and no-requested-origin states. Root owns inference, support/effect gates, family accounting and publication; this module verifies the forecast computation and identities only.

Final schema clarifications were incorporated into the generated fixtures before implementation: unassigned phase is the literal `outside_phase`; panel rows are ordered by origin and then market/matched/candidate. The evaluation target fence is source_end, which can be later than the final evaluation origin. Development targets must end by the development endpoint. Every supplied market date is at or before source_end; an unknown fifth-session endpoint is `target_not_mature`. Coverage precedence is incomplete_features, outside_phase, target_not_mature, missing_target, target_after_phase_cutoff, scored. No-fit schedule dates and training counts remain missing; requested_n retains the actual requested calendar count even when all features are incomplete.

Before implementation, generated tests will supply independent scalar/vectorized oracles for baseline, claims, target and model stages and a separately assembled synthetic pipeline payload. Corruptions will exercise same-day release selection, stale/unknown fallback, missing Saturdays, target shifts, maturity cutoffs, common cohorts, offsets, monthly identities, smearing, coefficients, predictions, QLIKE and unscored omissions. Expected values will not come from the producer or verifier under test. No additional source review, model screening or historical calculation is authorized here.
