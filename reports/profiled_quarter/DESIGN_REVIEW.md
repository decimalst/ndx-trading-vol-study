## Mathematical and numerical review

For the actual training design slopes X, normalized positive target q and penalty alpha=.01, the full criterion is `a + mean(Xb) + exp(-a) mean(q exp(-Xb)) + alpha ||b||²`. Eliminating the unpenalized intercept gives `a=log(mean(q exp(-Xb)))`. The resulting slope criterion is `1+a+mean(Xb)+alpha||b||²`, with gradient `mean(X)-weighted_mean(X)+.02b` and Hessian `weighted_covariance(X)+.02I`. The implementation retains the actual sample-mean term, uses the caller's target normalization, and starts from zero slopes without consulting producer coefficients. In exact arithmetic the regularized slope criterion is strictly convex. Its computed-gradient error bounds are correctly described as numerical residual bounds, not formal interval certificates.
