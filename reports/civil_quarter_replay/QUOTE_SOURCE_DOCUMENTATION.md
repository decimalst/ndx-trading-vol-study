## What official documentation establishes

The [US option-chain field definitions](https://www.optionsdx.com/option-chain-field-definitions/) identify `C_BID/C_ASK` and `P_BID/P_ASK` as call/put bid and ask prices. They distinguish Unix quote time, readable quote time, quote date, hour-of-day, underlying last price and daily traded volume. They do **not** specify a venue or consolidated feed, per-side update age, synchronized bid/ask/underlying sampling, correction policy, or fresh executable NBBO. The US page does not explicitly identify the readable timestamp's timezone or DST convention. Its DTE description mixes days and hours; the [FAQ examples](https://www.optionsdx.com/faq/) treat DTE as days. Exact maturity units and expiry-clock handling need clarification before a new selector is admitted. Crypto documentation is a different schema and cannot supply missing SPY conventions.

The [OptionsDX FAQ](https://www.optionsdx.com/faq/) states that datasets are distributed quarterly, roughly during the first two weeks following each quarter. It describes each row as a put/call pair at one strike and expiry. **A recorded quote timestamp is therefore not evidence that this archive was obtainable on the next session.** The FAQ describes the current delivery policy, not a dated historical release ledger. Nor does it establish that an independent contemporaneous feed could reproduce these exact archived values. Delaying a displayed EOD timestamp by one session does not solve either issue.

The public [terms page](https://www.optionsdx.com/terms/) retrieved here addresses final sales and refunds; it contains no explicit grant covering this third-party copy, private research, publication of derived aggregates, or redistribution. The [disclaimer](https://www.optionsdx.com/disclaimer/) disclaims guaranteed accuracy and completeness. That disclaimer supplies neither permission nor a quote-freshness guarantee. Absence of an explicit grant in these retrieved pages is an unresolved permission question, not a determination that every research use is prohibited.

## Existing parser does not supply the missing evidence

The frozen [normalizer](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/src/surface_data_study.py:101) requires a displayed 16:00 hour and readable-time pattern, valid reference dates, nonnegative uncrossed quotes and other fixed contract filters. It does not reconcile `QUOTE_UNIXTIME`, the date embedded in `QUOTE_READTIME`, and `QUOTE_DATE`, or prove timezone/DST consistency. It also does not establish the update age of the individual quotes. Its deterministic 30-day/ATM selectors are useful implementation precedents, not source-authentication evidence. No old parser or result was changed.

## Exact unresolved gates and next action

Before any new numerical extraction, obtain documentary confirmation from OptionsDX through its [published contact route](https://www.optionsdx.com/contact-us/) and, where necessary, the Kaggle uploader:

1. The license applicable to the **specific mirrored version**, its authorized provenance and conversion chain, and permission for the intended private analysis and proposed non-reconstructive outputs. If the mirror is not authorized, establish an authorized direct-source route; merely replacing a URL does not validate the old bytes.
2. The SPY EOD sampling rule and source feed; whether bid and ask are simultaneous snapshots, exchange-specific or consolidated; how stale, absent, corrected and carried-forward quotes are represented; and how underlying/Greeks/volume are timed. A documented snapshot may support a narrow recorded-width hypothesis without supporting execution, but that distinction must be fixed before numerical inspection.
3. Timezone/DST and unit definitions for all clock and maturity fields, plus sufficient evidence to specify exact cross-field consistency tests and early-close handling. No future realized calendar may retroactively move a predictor's measurement time.
4. Historical publication/delivery timing and revision policy. For the proposed one-session predictor, document a contemporaneously obtainable equivalent measurement. Otherwise the proposal remains blocked; switching to quarterly-lagged width would be a different hypothesis requiring a fresh design.

Only after documentary admission should a separately authorized, pretested bounded source audit reconcile the pinned bytes and fields, preserve unknowns, and establish common sample/support. No quotation-age heuristic, positive-volume test, MIT uploader label, or favorable forecast result can substitute for the missing documentation.
