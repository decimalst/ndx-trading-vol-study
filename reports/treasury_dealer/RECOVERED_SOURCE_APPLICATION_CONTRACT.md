# Additive application of recovered Treasury originals

Apply the existing, pretested identity and amount parsers to exactly three
previously unresolved events: December23,2019 (912828YZ7), December24,2019
(912828YY0), and December9,2020 (91282CAV3). For the first and third events,
the recovered competitive-result PDF must agree with its Internet Archive CDX
digest and dated release header, then reconcile with the already pinned
announcement PDF/XML and result XML. For YY0 the newly recovered December19
notice must be explicitly reviewed as a conditional reopening warning; retain
the existing paired announcement/result identities and amounts from those
originals, and verify the final unchanged five-year identity. The warning is
not itself evidence that the conditional reopening occurred.

Use the unchanged confirmed-history identity, result PDF/XML amount and
three-source offering parsers. Compare all sixteen bidder/accounting fields
and all three offering amounts exactly, under their existing units. No parser
adapter, alias, date rewrite, revised numeric source, or new allocation
definition is introduced. The 57-test recovered-source precheck includes the
existing independent parser contracts plus the nine new matched-market-context
tests; it passed before this application script was written.

Pin the new archived bytes, dated archive receipts, extraction/review evidence,
application code and test log before running. Authenticate the original terminal
checkpoint and its full artifact map before reading source contents. Decode the
same checked byte snapshots, save new per-event private outputs, and preserve
all old failed transport and reconciliation records. An original record's old
status never changes: these are additional evidence and additional outcomes.

The archived PDFs establish historical existence at their respective snapshot
times; their printed agency release dates remain the qualified daily clock
evidence, with the first usable close strictly later. Neither current retrieval
time nor an original Last-Modified field is a first-publication claim. This
stage admits source evidence only. It does not build a market feature panel,
register a predictive comparison, fit a model, or calculate a forecasting score.

January27,2020 (912828Z52) remains outside this three-event application. Its
cached primary text may be assessed separately for a clock-only disposition;
it must not supply numerical quantities or falsely acquire a PDF-byte identity
certificate. January2016's unresolved CUSIP conflict and the June2019 test
auction also remain unchanged.
