# Independent peak-age mathematics and complete-cohort verification

Prospective specification, 2026-09-08. `src/peak_age_verification.py` consumes supplied admitted source and output tables; it reads no historical files and imports no producer mathematical or scheduling routines. Its public pipeline signature is:

`verify_pipeline(daily, iv, protocol, features, targets, feature_states, panel, fits, application_states, support)`.

The helper independently reconstructs the complete bounded source-feature-target-state tables, every monthly common mature training cohort, every fitted mean/baseline/depth/scalar stage, every full application including unscored origins, all scored predictions and primitive squared losses, and the complete support audit. Source admission, protocol/freeze enforcement, independent hypothesis inference, ledger and final publication identity checks belong to the separate full entry. Passing this helper alone cannot establish predictive usefulness or authorize promotion.

## Independent reconstruction and exact clocks

The original fifteen controls are reconstructed from supplied raw SPX OHLC and aligned VIX/VIX9D/VVIX inputs. The lag, GK floor, overnight addition, rolling widths, I/R definitions and weekday indicators are unchanged. Raw close returns use independent log-close differences; the feature comparison uses declared relative1e-10/absolute1e-12 tolerances and exact unknown masks. Missing observations retain their source-calendar positions; observed nonpositive/infinite prices, malformed complete OHLC ranges and invalid numerical operations fail.

For each origin i>=252 the peak window is exactly closes i-252 through i-1. The independent implementation loops over that literal window, obtains the exact stored-price maximum and selects its last equal position. Peak positions and age counts are integers; age is their literal ratio to251 and compares exactly. New depth/window log ratios use 100-digit Decimal logarithms of the exact binary floats, independently of the producer's frexp/log1p implementation. Squared depth is one checked multiplication. All new-coordinate signs and zero masks must agree exactly, so the generic comparison tolerance cannot hide a tiny nonzero drawdown replaced by zero.

The full state schema is origin_position, cutoff_position, window_start_position, window_end_position, peak_position, peak_age_sessions, window_start_date, window_end_date, peak_date, feature_cutoff_date and window_complete. Nullable Int64 position/age dtypes and boolean completeness are exact. Missing windows retain available positional endpoints while peak metadata stays unknown. The sole21-session target is independently reconstructed from endpoint closes with Decimal log ratios and relative1e-10/absolute1e-13 comparison; target_end and available_date remain the exact observed session i+21.

Native naive midnight ms/us/ns source indices and declared date columns compare by checked lossless nanosecond conversion and roundtrip, with exact NaT masks and instants. String/object dates, timezones, subday values and overflow are rejected. Schema, row/column order, index names and every nondate dtype remain exact. The producer's canonical ns output does not require source files to be rewritten. Source and IV date ceilings, origin/phase bounds and the protected-period fence are checked before reconstruction.

## Independent model objectives and retained conditional offsets

The common design contains nineteen raw columns: the original fifteen plus peak_age, drawdown, drawdown_sq and window_return. Training-only I/R curvature produces the seventeen-column baseline and twenty-column depth design, both including the intercept. The independent helper checks all sixteen/nineteen nuisance population scales above1e-12 and fits an unpenalized training-mean intercept with .01 squared standardized-slope penalty. Its augmented least-squares solve avoids the producer's normal equations. No unused hinge feature or additional target is fitted.

Saved settings, model column lists, counts, zero-branch status and schema are exact. JSON booleans cannot substitute for numerical coefficients, even inside an otherwise numeric list. Saved centers/scales/objectives/gradients use relative1e-10/absolute1e-12 comparison; saved coefficients use relative1e-7/absolute1e-12. The independently solved optimum and a separate recomputation of the saved coefficient's objective and full gradient must both pass. Half-gradient maxima are finite, nonnegative and <=1e-10. Predictions are independently checked at relative1e-8/absolute1e-12.

The scalar stage conditions on the saved depth coefficients after their independent validation. It replays that precise current monthly fit on its original mature training rows; it does not substitute the independently solved depth optimum or historical issuance errors. Age is centered by compensated mean, with an exact first-value center for constant ages, and keeps scale one. The scalar optimum is solved by a one-column augmented least-squares system; the saved closed-form coefficient is independently checked against it and its objective/full gradient. Required products and compensated reductions reject nonzero underflow rather than entering a zero branch accidentally.

Exact-constant and exactly balanced-zero stages retain coefficient0 and unchanged depth predictions, including signed-zero bits. A nonzero coefficient uses the declared addition even when a particular query's centered age is zero; no extra bit-preservation gate is imposed on that branch. Both coefficient signs are allowed. There is no extra intercept, joint nuisance refit, age standardization or residualized-age projection. A generated age-inside-nuisance-span fixture demonstrates that the sequential stage can retune ridge shrinkage; this is an accepted property of the declared algorithm, not proof of orthogonal information.

## Every scheduled and scored observation

Months are selected from common feature-complete origins inside the declared phase/origin fences before future-label filtering. The first such origin fixes the fit; all earlier common-complete labels available by its preceding observed-session close belong to that month's training cohort. At least1,000 rows are required. Exact ordered training and application ISO-date lists in every fit are checked independently, including wholly or partly unscored applications. All four arms share those rows and clocks.

Full application-state columns are origin, fit_origin, feature_cutoff_date, train_n and pred_mean/pred_baseline/pred_depth/pred_peak_age. Scored panel columns are origin, fit_origin, feature_cutoff_date, target_end, available_date, horizon, phase, model, prediction, y, loss and train_n. The helper independently rebuilds their memberships, ordered rows and model predictions. Scored target bits must additionally copy the checked target table exactly, and scored prediction bits must copy the corresponding full application state exactly. These are transport identities, separate from solver tolerances. Primitive squared errors are then recomputed exactly from those retained values.

Support is reconstructed with exactly calendar_rows, common_feature_rows, monthly_fits, application_origins, scored_origins, unscored_origins, ordered unscored_origin_dates and phases. Each phase has n, first_origin and last_origin, with None endpoints only when empty. Changing a count, cohort, clock, unscored prediction, coefficient, label or required schema fails the whole helper; no row, month, model or nuisance is removed to achieve agreement.

For n scored origins, a applications and k schedules, returned counts distinguish forecasts_verified=4n, primitive_squared_losses_verified=4n, application_origins_verified=a, application_predictions_verified=4a, common_scored_origins=n, unscored_origins_verified=a-n, monthly_fits_verified=k, new_nuisance_fits_verified=2k, new_scalar_fits_verified=k and training_mean_fits_verified=k. Source reconstruction, exact support and per-month independent model/scalar audit records are returned separately. Independently solved optima are verification evidence and never replace producer outputs.

## Prewritten tests, corrections and scope

`tests/test_peak_age_verification.py` was written in stages before their corresponding implementations. The initial missing-module RED is retained, followed by producer-challenge and full-pipeline RED logs. Primitive tests cover extreme/near-equal positive ratios, exact ties, endpoints, missing windows, causal features/targets, one-origin algebraic distinction, analytic ridge/scalar fixtures, valid zero branches, invalid domains and the ridge-shrinkage attribution counterexample.

The first primitive implementation exposed one test-oracle issue: an exact equality assertion compared Decimal log(10/12) with a rounded binary division followed by log, differing by2.8e-17. Only that synthetic assertion was corrected to its intended fifteen-decimal comparison; no source arithmetic or empirical tolerance changed. The first full fixture exposed the root pipeline's unsupported NumPy dtype.unit access; its owner preserved the original failing tests and changed only date-unit introspection. Subsequent generated integration exercises that corrected boundary with actual producer models.

Additional prewritten regressions falsified draft verifier behavior before their fixes: nonzero compensated-mean underflow; tiny nonzero depth hidden by a generic float tolerance; a boolean substituted inside a zero coefficient list; and one-ULP scored-target/application-copy mismatches hidden by solver tolerance. A final signed-zero fixture caught an overbroad draft bit-copy rule for nonzero coefficients; the helper now uses literal addition on that branch and reserves exact copying for the declared zero coefficient branches. Their RED logs remain. Fixes enforce the originally declared arithmetic, type and retained-table identity contracts. They do not relax a mathematical objective, solver threshold or gate.

The full transport fixture uses1,420 generated OHLC/IV observations, actual four-arm producer fits on >=1,000 mature common rows, separate original source units ms/us/ns, Parquet table transport and JSON audit transport. The verifier runs unmocked reconstruction and augmented solves while producer entrypoints are patched to raise if called. It verifies final and phase-boundary unscored applications. Further mutations challenge complete training/application lists, all model audits, support, clocks, labels and coherent scored/unscored predictions. No historical values or support counts participate.

The final26 tests passed in42.986 seconds, recorded in `verification_final_26_green.txt`, and scoped Ruff passed in `verification_final_lint.txt`; earlier23-,24- and25-test green logs remain alongside their falsifiers. This document does not claim the historical trial is supported, numerically verified or predictive. Full repository tests, freeze, registration, historical source admission and the separate publication verifier remain required.
