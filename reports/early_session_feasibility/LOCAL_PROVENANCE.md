# Local source feasibility for post-09:35 SPX risk

**Source-blocked in the bounded local inventory.** None of the inspected, previously audited sources supplies timestamped SPX index prices that can separate the period from 09:35 America/New_York onward over the proposed original training and evaluation history. The local daily SPX prices and daily high-frequency-derived risk summaries cannot instantiate that target. This is a documentary finding about the inspected source set, not an exhaustive claim about every file on the machine or every possible provider.

This review supports the adaptive, unregistered [next-direction proposal](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/reports/issued_calibration/NEXT_RESEARCH_DIRECTION.md). It does not register or run a new experiment, alter wave 19, or authorize a substitute asset or shorter sample.

## Scope and inspection limits

The review used existing source audits, protocols and manifests, a filename-only inventory in the known raw/research-path and HARNet/RiskLab cache directories, and the Arrow schemas from the footers of two named SPX parquet files. It also checked existence of the previously documented NQ minute CSV. It did **not** read market rows, parquet column statistics, new observation counts, protected-period values, new feature/target measurements or associations. No downloads, acquisitions, source reparsing, model fits or changes to prior files occurred. Coverage dates below are inherited documentary metadata, not newly reconstructed coverage.

The two footer schemas were:

- [SPX daily prices](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/data/research_paths/spx_daily.parquet): `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`, `date`; numeric prices and a timestamp-typed date column.
- [Oxford SPX measures](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/data/raw/oxford_man_spx.parquet): `bv`, `rv5`, `date`; two numeric measures and a timestamp-typed date column.

A timestamp storage type does not establish intraday observation frequency, quote time or publication time. Frequency and field meaning here come from the source documentation.

## Exact unresolved gate and useful next action

Daily OHLC cannot reconstruct the high and low after 09:35 by subtracting the opening shock or the first five minutes. Daily realized variance also cannot be split without separately supplied interval observations or compatible interval components. Neither a field named `open` nor a daily `date` establishes information observable at the chosen cutoff.

The useful next action is a bounded **primary-provider documentary review** for timestamped cash-SPX index observations or suitable intraday bars, before acquiring or parsing values. An admissible source plan must resolve:

1. Cash-index identity and construction, historical coverage for the original phases and their preceding training history, and explicit private-research permission. SPY, ES or NQ would be a separately justified asset/target change.
2. Observation time versus bar-start/bar-end time, dissemination or availability convention, timezone/DST, revisions, and a deterministic cutoff sampling rule. A completed bar bearing a 09:35 label cannot be assumed known at 09:35. Index levels must not be described as executable quotes.
3. A reference value observable by the fixed cutoff and high/low/close built only over the subsequent interval, including session/early-close boundaries, opening construction, missing observations and stale observations. If the reference observation is older than the cutoff, that alone does not certify its value as an exact interval-opening measurement; the convention must be resolved prospectively.
4. The same post-cutoff historical risk measure for the trailing reference `K_t`, with its matched baseline controls fixed before calculating any values. The new historical denominator must not masquerade as information from the current early-session shock.

Until those gates are met, there is no local source-complete experiment to register for this question. Reopening aggregate-value archives, silently shortening the history, or substituting a full-day nowcast would not resolve the stated remaining-session target. No new numerical work is needed to establish the present resolution/asset mismatch.
