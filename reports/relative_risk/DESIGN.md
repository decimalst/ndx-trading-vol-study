# Relative intraday risk: prospective experiment contract

This ninth bounded wave asks whether trailing joint intraday correlation adds information when forecasting the next-session log risk ratio of **QQQ ETF versus the SPX price index**. The [blinded novelty review](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/reports/calendar_variance/NEXT_RELATIVE_RISK_DESIGN.md) found no such relative target in the inspected implementations, while documenting overlap with earlier absolute-risk, cross-asset, IV and memory studies. The proposed method supplies one joint statistic from existing data, not new raw information or an exact Nasdaq-100-index comparison.

The [protocol](/Users/byrons/code/trading-vol/ndx-vol-experiment/relative_risk.yaml), measurement gate, synthetic tests and independent verifier are specified before new numerical measurements or fits. Two registered contrasts compare the correlation augmentation against the strong baseline and the same-row historical mean. The source/date audit establishes 1,254 potentially mature training rows at January 4, 2016, using a December 31, 2015 cutoff. This is date support only; it does not establish measurement quality or predictive value.

## Measurement admission before fitting

Use the full bounded observed SPX calendar through October 20, 2025, preserving every reference date before rolling. On each asset separately, inspect every observed complete raw OHLC row aligned to that calendar **before any derived correlation, feature, target or sample mask**. Missing quotes remain unknown; nonpositive or infinite observed prices, inconsistent complete ranges, or nonfinite calculated risk invalidate the run.

The fixed intraday Garman–Klass calculation is `0.5 log(H/L)^2 − (2 log(2)−1) log(C/O)^2`. Every observed result must be strictly above the existing `1e-10` floor. Equality and smaller values activate the gate. Any activation stops the entire registered design as insufficient measurement; it cannot be hidden by a missing IV value or later training/sample exclusion. Save the per-asset audit before requiring the gate to pass. No target, numerical feature or model is constructed after gate failure, and both hypotheses remain unevaluable with p=1. A separate verifier can certify this terminal state as `VERIFIED_INSUFFICIENT_MEASUREMENT`, distinct from a negative forecast result.

If the gate passes, the response is `log(GK_QQQ[next SPX session]) − log(GK_SPX[next SPX session])`, requiring both observations on that exact next date. Zero and negative log ratios are valid. The target is a conditional mean of a log OHLC-risk ratio under squared error; it is not a ratio of expected variances, measured high-frequency integrated variance, or a hedged portfolio's variance. Both observations mature at the target session's close.

## One addition to strong controls

The baseline has 27 columns: intercept; each asset's strict 1/5/22-session means of intraday GK and total GK-plus-raw-overnight risk, logged after averaging; each asset's 1/5/22 means of negative raw close-return parts; log VXN, log VIX, log(VIX9D/VIX), log VVIX; and four entry-weekday indicators. Every market predictor ends at the preceding observed SPX session. Raw overnight and close-return measurements require the asset's actual source predecessor to equal that preceding reference date. Missing/mismatched dates are never bridged by compressing an intersection.

The candidate adds only strict trailing 22-session Pearson correlation between QQQ and SPX raw open-to-close log returns. Each window needs all 22 paired observations. Exact constant inputs and zero centered denominators are unknown. The implementation checks exact constancy before centering: a pre-fit test exposed how repeated nonzero values can otherwise acquire a tiny artificial variance through rounding. Finite values with absolute correlation at most `1+1e-12` are retained as computed; larger violations fail. There is no epsilon denominator, clipping, alternate window or transformed-correlation arm.

All models share the 28 complete inputs, mature finite labels, fit dates and scored origins. Monthly expanding fits begin on the first feature-complete origin, independently of future query-label presence. At least 1,000 mature common training labels and nonzero population scales above `1e-12` for all 27 nonintercept columns are required. Fit the fixed normalized ridge objective, mean squared error plus 0.01 times squared standardized slopes, with an unpenalized intercept and no target-standard-deviation normalization. A direct solve must satisfy the full first-order equations within `1e-10`; there is no fallback.

The prewritten algebraic test establishes that, with identical rows, inputs, scaling and penalty, a ridge forecast of the component log-risk difference equals the difference of the two component ridge forecasts. Such a repackaging is not a separate information arm. Likewise, adding a log risk or IV ratio already in the controls' linear span could change ridge geometry without adding information; it is excluded from this experiment.

## Evidence gates and audit

Development is 2016–2019 with labels complete by December 31, 2019. Evaluation is January 2, 2020 through October 17, 2025 with labels complete by October 20. The sealed November 3 onward boundary is unchanged. Both comparisons must reduce MSE by at least **0.25% in both phases**, improve both fixed evaluation subperiods, pass wave Holm at `0.05/(9×10)`, and pass cumulative Holm across **110 enumerated hypotheses** at 0.05. This tracked family is not the repository's complete lifetime trial count.

Each phase uses 99,999 circular block resamples at blocks 21, 63 and 126, plus Bartlett HAC126. Retain the largest two-sided p-value across methods and then across phases. At least 127 paired observations are required without reducing a bandwidth. Report both comparisons, absolute MSE gaps, relative point gains, intervals, nominal minimum detectable effects and all annual/fixed-period diagnostics. Zero control MSE cannot define the relative-gain comparison and aborts the wave.

Independent verification separately reconstructs bounded sources, the measurement gate, all market/correlation inputs, signed labels, training membership and forecasts. An augmented least-squares ridge replay and full first-order residual check verify coefficients and predictions under predeclared tolerances. Synthetic tests also cover source and future-date mutations, missing windows, scaling identities, retained zero targets and publication failures. Any registered measurement, fitting, scoring, publication or verification failure retains both comparisons at p=1, with partial scores confined to unpublished diagnostics. No empirical adjustment or retry is permitted.

## Limits and retained sources

The date audit found 4,226 matching observed dates, no missing/extra QQQ dates inside the SPX span, and no later predecessor mismatch. Their equality does not certify completeness against an exchange-session ledger or synchronized auction observations. QQQ has extra pre-2009 history; the first SPX reference row has no matching prior SPX close and is unknown for prior-dependent histories.

The target's within-session ratios cancel common multiplicative units and avoid directly including QQQ's previous-close distribution gap. ETF tracking, index construction, source revisions and vendor conventions remain. Raw overnight/close-return controls still carry intersession distribution limitations. VXN proxies the QQQ underlying, while VIX/VIX9D concern SPX; early VIX9D history is back-calculated. All feeds are archival and exact historical release latency is unverified. No trading-profit or allocation claim follows from a passing prediction result. Reused history remains exploratory despite statistical correction.
