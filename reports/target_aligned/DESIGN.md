Use one common numerical unit `k=mean(D)` per training fit, then `d=D/k` and `y=Y/k`. All new scalar reductions use compensated `math.fsum(values)/n`. This preserves uniform original product MSE up to one positive constant; it introduces no observation weights. Fit `a=project_[−.995,.995](mean(d*y)/mean(d*d))`. Hold that intercept fixed and set `headroom=.995-abs(a)`, `w=headroom*d*phi`, and `b=project_[−1,1](mean(w*(y-a*d))/mean(w*w))`. This constraint makes them compatible with a positive-definite two-asset matrix; it does not establish calibration of the marginal forecasts. There is no grid, penalty selection, local optimization, or application clipping.
