# Independent crossed-calibration verification

Implemented before new historical calibration fits. Public API is `verify(archive, produced, minimum_train=252, calendar=None)`. The registered execution supplies the original full reference calendar and uses the fixed 252-pair floor; smaller floors are for invented fixtures only. No producer calibration function, history selector, scoring function or monthly pipeline is imported to establish expected values.

The canonical archive must have unique increasing origins, native source-bounded dates, genuinely issued forecasts, finite original locations, positive variances, bounded original correlations and explicit scoring eligibility. Observed returns can be signed or zero; missing targets remain missing. For each original first-issued monthly fit, the verifier independently selects every earlier issued pair whose target and availability are both no later than the original prior-session cutoff. The history expands. Earlier unsupported whole months remain warmup. Every original application in each supported month is retained, including unscored applications. Original locations, variances, dates, offsets and training identities are copied exactly. The verified input boundary is the authenticated original forecast archive, not a new fit of the original market models.

When a calendar is supplied, previous-session training cutoffs, next-session target/availability and global modulo-five offsets are checked against it. Without a calendar the proof explicitly records inherited original-calendar validation; it does not claim to reconstruct missing calendar positions. Historical data authentication, original feature/marginal correctness, and the archive-to-original-artifact bridge remain caller-owned and separately verified.

For each asset, independent normal scores are obtained from the original t8 standardized residual. Population mean and standard deviation on the common mature issued pairs give location a and scale b. Both scales must exceed 1e-12. The corrected normal coordinate is v=(w-a)/b. The independent inverse t8 transform uses the smaller normal tail; for large normal coordinates it solves the t8 log-tail identity in log magnitude, without clipping a CDF to an endpoint. The four dependency certificates are independently checked using frozen `verify_joint_copula_forecasts.verify_dependence`: all Gaussian stationary roots/endpoints and the entire t8 interval-certificate partition are reconstructed. This reuses an independent verifier, not the fitted producer objective or optimizer as an oracle.

The verifier reconstructs each original and calibrated log marginal density, both PIT diagnostics, both normal-score diagnostics, four negative full joint log densities and all seven signed contrasts. The calibrated log marginal is log(f0)-0.5*(v²-w²)-log(b), including the exact Jacobian. Gaussian dependence is evaluated directly on w or v; t8 dependence is evaluated on z or inverse_t8(Phi(v)). No transformed-variable pseudo-likelihood is substituted. The original marginal parameters remain the fixed input components; the calibrated distribution need not retain their mean or variance, and its variance need not be finite. Density normalization does not prove calibration of higher moments or distributional shape.

The seven contrasts are original t8-minus-Gaussian, calibrated t8-minus-Gaussian, calibrated gap minus original gap, calibration's joint-loss change under Gaussian, its joint-loss change under t8, and each asset's calibrated-minus-original negative marginal log density. The interaction is positive when the t8 advantage attenuates. It does not alone show that the marginal correction helped. This module verifies per-row values and the common chronology; it does not perform inference, multiplicity correction or promote a lead.

Dates, ordered cohorts, original forecasts and discrete identities are exact. New parameters, PIT values, normal coordinates, full densities and contrasts use absolute tolerance 1e-10 plus relative tolerance 1e-8. Fixed independent certificate tolerances and the 1e-8 global-gap limit are inherited unchanged. Any mismatch raises. Success reports status VERIFIED, monthly_fits_verified, dependence_fits_verified, calibration_parameters_verified, applications_verified, scored_origins, contrasts_verified7, largest_independent_global_gap and the calendar-verification scope. Numerical failures in the producer are not waived by this verifier's independently implemented wider log-tail inversion domain.

## Prewritten generated evidence

Eleven test methods were written before the verifier module. `VERIFICATION_RED.log` retains the actual missing-module failure. Tests cover population rather than sample scaling, degenerate and invalid scales, independent high-precision inverse tails, Jacobian normalization by quadrature, unchanged units and identity calibration, direct multivariate density formulas, all seven contrasts, actual producer replay, Parquet/JSON serialization, exact mature history, warmup and unscored issuance, current-month outcome blindness, every output group, and corrupted dates, cohorts and global certificates.

The first primitive run had four passes and one test-oracle failure: using `norm.cdf(+8)` before inversion had already lost upper-tail precision. The retained failure shows a mismatch against the symmetric negative-tail answer. Only the oracle was corrected to signed `t.isf(norm.sf(abs(w)), 8)`; its tolerances were unchanged. The first integrated run reached the independently verified fits and then exposed four newly supplied normal-score output columns absent from the original schema description. Those four columns were added to independent reconstruction and corruption coverage. An invented integer-count mutation was also changed from fractional +0.03 to integer +1 so pandas could construct the intended corrupted input. First-run evidence is retained separately. No fitted model, mathematical expectation, chronology, support floor or tolerance was changed to obtain agreement.

Final generated check: **11 tests pass in 2.936 seconds**. Scoped Ruff and format checks pass. The tests use only invented dates, forecasts and outcomes; no historical market values, calibration cohorts, new fits or scores were accessed. No original frozen source or report was edited.

## Critic simulation read-only review

The complete supplied `critic_scripts/README.md` and `sim.py` were read under `reports/joint_copula/critique_review/`; neither was executed. The simulation uses 30 repetitions, seed7 and 2462 pairs. It generates independent draws with saved per-origin correlations and exact t8 marginals, then fits one scalar correlation per family on each whole simulated sample and scores that same sample. This mixture of per-origin generating correlations and in-sample refitting does not reproduce monthly issued forecasts or marginal misspecification. The between-repetition standard deviation is a simulation diagnostic, not a bound on the actual score contrast. Its numbers are not model-selection targets or acceptance thresholds for this study.
