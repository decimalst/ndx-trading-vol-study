# Independent range-alert verification, before historical construction

The verifier independently reconstructs the new daily event, 24 raw predictors, 26-column training designs, both fitted probability models, every application state, issued Brier scores, all four phase comparisons, and the complete 129-hypothesis accounting. Its tests were written and their initial missing-module failure saved before implementation. All test inputs are generated; no new historical feature, event count, fit or score was inspected during this design.

The two original successful records and their original code/input/publication hashes are checked without invoking historical model fits, inference or writing entry points. Eleven fixed input files are copied from single hash-checked byte snapshots into an isolated directory before the frozen independent tail-study source parser runs. That parser bounds dates before numerical CSV conversion and retains the archived SKEW identity and raw/derived comparison. New source-audit descriptions change the interpretation only. Current private files from the preceding study are preserved by the new manifest; they are not misrepresented as carrying a previously published output digest or as having been refitted here.

For each complete OHLC bar, the new location variable is the square of its logarithmic closing location inside its high–low range. Observed operand pairs and arithmetic are checked even when another bar field is missing. There is no clipping. A zero range leaves location unknown while otherwise known risk and event labels remain available. Independent feature construction reproduces the legacy rolling controls, adds the four signed-return/square nuisance controls, and shifts every market input by one full reference session. Computed failure in a complete rolling window is rejected rather than converted into source missingness.

The binary event compares next-session floored-GK-plus-overnight-square risk with twice the mean of precisely the 22 sessions ending before the origin. The new reference is checked `math.fsum(window)/22`, followed by checked multiplication by two. Exact equality is zero; any missing reference or target risk is unknown. Binary targets and their missingness are compared exactly, not with a floating-point tolerance. Event labels do not depend on location or implied-volatility completeness.

Monthly dates are the first feature-complete applications before future-label filtering. Every training label must be available at the previous observed session; all fixed training, phase and slice class minima are checked before optimization. All 25 baseline slopes use training population scales exceeding `1e-12`. The three curvature centers and location center are training-only. Exact-constant location retains a zero centered column and canonical zero scalar coefficient.

The independent baseline solves the analytic normalized logistic gradient using SciPy `root(method="hybr")`, starting with 26 zeros, analytic Hessian Jacobian, `xtol=1e-10`, `maxfev=2000`, and `factor=1`. Both solver success and the original full gradient at most `1e-8 + 1e-12` are mandatory. Status, message, evaluation counts and the recomputed gradient norm are recorded. There is no restart or fallback. The producer's distinct fixed initialization and Newton/Armijo method are checked from its saved audit.

The scalar correction holds the producer's independently checked baseline logit fixed. A separate monotone-gradient Brent solve uses the declared symmetric bound `(mean(abs(centered_E))+1)/.02`, tolerances `1e-12`/`1e-14` and maximum 200 iterations. Independent coefficient/probability comparisons use `rtol=1e-7, atol=1e-6`; saved-coefficient probability replay uses `rtol=1e-10, atol=1e-12`. Finite probability domains are checked before tolerances. Brier scores replay exactly from the issued probabilities.

Recent frequency is reconstructed from explicit compensated weighted sums, not the producer recurrence: seed mass decays through the full session clock, and each known post-seed label receives its predetermined decayed weight. Seed cutoff and last consumed availability are separate. Missing labels decay both sums; feature gaps and unscored labels never reset the clock. Every application, including wholly unscored months, is checked. The fixed elapsed-session roundoff envelope retains exact zero/sign masks; saved probability is exactly replayed from saved numerator/mass.

Independent inference retains all four phase/control comparisons, 199,999 draws at each fixed block length, HAC126, absolute `.0005` effect gates, both later slice signs, wave Holm2 and cumulative Holm129. Calibration in the large and nominal detectable effect are descriptive. Output, metrics and ledger byte snapshots are checked again at completion and their exact hashes are serialized in the verification record. Any verifier exception replaces only this wave's canonical publication with both registered p-values equal to one, preserves optional diagnostics, and appends both failure events before optional backup operations.

Synthetic checks cover exact threshold ties, missing/partial bars, source and metadata tampering, independent derivatives/root termination, constant location, full-clock decay, literal probability bounds, nonfinite failure metrics, exact binary comparison and the complete entry-point artifact paths. Generated producer-to-verifier integration additionally covers all monthly fits, unscored applications, state and coefficient mutation rejection, and independent phase inference. Earlier frozen files are not edited.
