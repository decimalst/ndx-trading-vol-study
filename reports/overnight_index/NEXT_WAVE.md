# Next experiment after three completed signal-search waves

Status: prospective design and source work only. **Wave four is not registered
or scored.** The completed cumulative family remains 78 contrasts, including

No adjustment to an earlier gate or frozen prediction is proposed.

## First priority: planned announcements and overnight second moments

The next distinct mechanism is uncertainty about a scheduled release: a date
known in advance may help predict the size of the overnight move even when its
sign is unpredictable. Use CPI and payroll original before-open plans, with
scheduled FOMC after-open plans as a separate timing control. An after-open
effect could reflect anticipation and is not proof of preopen release risk.
The existing date-only retrospective CSVs are insufficient for this design.

The [feasibility report](MACRO_OVERNIGHT_VARIANCE_FEASIBILITY.md) specifies a
positive log-link model for squared adjusted overnight log returns, scored by
`log(h) + q/h`. This accepts exact zero squared returns. The target is a
conditional second moment, not observed integrated variance or exact cash
profit. Proposed controls are a strong own-history/IV/weekday/holding-duration
model and the same-sample historical mean. Three singleton additions against
both controls create six contrasts; none may be dropped because it fails.

Four [official archived-release snapshots](BLS_PLAN_CAPTURE_FEASIBILITY.md)
establish a workable capture route. Next assemble the remaining bounded CPI
and payroll documents from official release indexes, in publication order,
covering enough pre-2016 history for 1,000 complete initial training labels and
all scored years through the October 2025 fence. Keep the exact extraction bytes,
hash, URL, source kind, capture time, stated publication time and original
next-plan time. These are current tool-rendered official-document extracts,
not original HTML bytes or historical web vintages. Do not relabel their hashes.

Freeze the policy before collecting the full ledger: retain the next plan
printed in each preceding monthly release, including later-cancelled plans;
never choose a source retrospectively using the actual event date. Missing or
ambiguous plans stay unknown. Preserve plan identities and explicit coverage
intervals so lack of a record is not automatically a non-event. Use advance
annual FOMC schedules without injecting emergency decisions.

The held interval also needs a genuinely predetermined session schedule: Good
Friday, early closes and DST affect which announcement can occur before the
next opening. Use a verified planned-calendar ledger or register a simpler
explicit calendar-time predictor before testing. A future realized closure may
change a target's actual ending but cannot retroactively change a predictor.
Do not silently substitute the next observed QQQ date for a planned opening.

After the source and timing contracts pass independent tests, freeze the full
numerical protocol. The current proposal is an absolute paired-score improvement
of at least 0.005 in both phases and against both controls, negative gaps in
both evaluation subperiods and adjustment-event sensitivity, and conservative
dependence-aware inference. It is a proposed effect threshold chosen without
event results; negative raw proper scores must not become percentage gains.
As wave four, six contrasts would receive Holm at `0.05/(4*5) = 0.0025` and the
full 84-contrast Holm check at 0.05. Use 49,999 bootstrap draws if retaining
blocks 21/63/126: their smallest attainable p-value, 0.00002, is below one-tenth
of the most stringent wave threshold. The old 19,999-draw budget is too coarse
for that declared resolution rule. Fix seeds, optimizer, convergence checks,
and failure accounting before fitting.

## Second priority: better realized measurements

The [HARNet overlap audit](HARNET_EXTENSION_NUMERIC_AUDIT.md) establishes
measurement agreement within fixed tolerance for the seven original markets,
with 397 additional SPX dates through 2020-01-31. Its literal `rv5` and neutral
`rsv` fields can support a separately specified extension; it lacks the old
kernel and subsampled-RV fields. A later-period extension cannot erase failed
development gates or turn the strongest earlier test into an untouched winner.
Keep original source bytes and old forecasts unchanged. Historical publication
vintages and current redistribution rights remain unverified.

The [Risk Lab SPY audit](RISKLAB_MEASUREMENT_AUDIT.md) offers a broader, separate
ETF target, including 2020–2025. Native squared annualized volatility would avoid
an unsupported daily-variance conversion. Before fitting, resolve or explicitly
bound the session, publication-lag and historical-vintage assumptions; retain
calendar gaps and fix targets, controls and candidates. Research-only source
terms permit the present assessment, not deployment or investment use. Do not
replace the primary estimator after inspecting another field's performance.

Neither source audit registers another forecast hypothesis or provides evidence
that a candidate predicts future returns or volatility. The next useful
empirical run starts only after its own complete protocol, provenance checks
and independent synthetic contracts are ready.
